## Proposed intake and continuation workflow

**PROPOSED:** copy every supplied chapter original under `chapters/originals/` using its source filename. Hash before and after copying; do not re-encode or normalize its text. Keep earlier versions in separate files; author approval, not modification time or filename alone, determines the current version.

**PROPOSED:** record each version's source, byte count, SHA-256, approval evidence and relation to other drafts. Keep chapter summaries separate from chapter originals.

**PROPOSED:** before writing a continuation, maintain a separate entry for events actually narrated; screen chronology; room revelations and who learned what; injuries and physical state; relationship state; unresolved threads; and the author's approved next chapter scope. None of these entries is prefilled with imagined events.
