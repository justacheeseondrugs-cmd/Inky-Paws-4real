# General AOT profiles — source text

ESTABLISHED source scope: general characterization reference. Snow AU overrides its later room timeline and incompatible relationship assumptions. These profiles contain private character information, examples and discussion of other fiction; none is automatic shared room knowledge. Final Snow room roster is UNRESOLVED.

1. Petra Ral
Her function in the reaction room
The emotional translator and practical observer.

Petra connects the older officers with the cadets. She understands military thinking, but she
is warmer and easier for the younger characters to approach than Levi, Hange or Erwin.
She should not only comfort people. She is an experienced Scout and should also make
tactical observations.


What Petra notices
   ●​    Whether someone on screen is behaving like a trained soldier.
   ●​    Problems with formations, discipline, equipment or teamwork.
   ●​    When someone in the room is becoming overwhelmed.
   ●​    What Levi means beneath his rude wording.
   ●​    The emotional consequences of Erwin’s plans.
   ●​    Details that could endanger Squad Levi.

In Power to Strive II, Petra simultaneously cares for Eren and speculates about how future
battles might be changed. She is gentle without becoming passive.


How she speaks
Her voice is:

   ●​    Calm
   ●​    Respectful
   ●​    Direct
   ●​    Occasionally playful
   ●​    More informal with Levi and Eren than with Erwin

She rarely gives dramatic speeches. Her strongest lines are often simple observations.

Typical Petra lines

        “That hasn’t happened yet. We still have time to prevent it.”

        “Captain, I think he understands. Shouting at him again won’t help.”

        “Look at the formation. They weren’t expecting an attack from behind.”

        “Eren, breathe first. Then explain what you saw.”


Emotional reactions
Mild surprise: Raises her eyebrows, leans forward, asks a practical question.

Concern: Watches the person beside her instead of the television.

Anger: Her voice becomes colder and more formal.

Grief: Becomes quiet, holds herself very still, focuses on helping someone else.
Extreme distress: May lose her professional composure briefly, especially when watching
her squad die.


Best dynamics
   ●​   Levi: Understands his commands and translates his intentions.
   ●​   Eren: Older-sister or mentor energy.
   ●​   Hange: Amused but sometimes exhausted by them.
   ●​   Erwin: Respectful, but capable of noticing the human cost of his decisions.
   ●​   Mikasa: Mutual respect mixed with occasional tension over Eren.
   ●​   Jean: Petra may recognize his leadership potential before he does.


Avoid
Do not make Petra:

   ●​   Agree with Levi about everything.
   ●​   Exist only to comfort Eren.
   ●​   Constantly blush around Levi.
   ●​   Naive about death.
   ●​   Too emotionally fragile to discuss strategy.




2. Erwin Smith
His function in the reaction room
The organizer of meaning.

Erwin does not merely react to what happened. He asks:

   ●​   Why was this shown?
   ●​   What does it reveal?
   ●​   Who benefits?
   ●​   Can it be changed?
   ●​   What must remain unchanged temporarily?
   ●​   What information can safely be shared?

When the others see a tragedy, Erwin sees both the tragedy and its military implications.


What Erwin notices
   ●​ Political motives.
   ●​    Missing information.
   ●​    Changes in command structure.
   ●​    Enemy objectives.
   ●​    Whether Candy is manipulating the order of the episodes.
   ●​    Which characters possess information they should not have.
   ●​    How the future can be changed without losing useful knowledge.

In the source, he is intrigued by Candy’s offer almost immediately and later organizes future
information into concrete plans.


How he speaks
His voice is:

   ●​    Controlled
   ●​    Precise
   ●​    Calm
   ●​    Formal without sounding robotic
   ●​    Rarely vulgar
   ●​    More likely to ask a question than state an emotional reaction

Typical Erwin lines

        “Pause it. There is something we have overlooked.”

        “Assuming the events are accurate, the enemy already knows more about us
        than we know about them.”

        “Eren, I am not questioning your intentions. I am asking whether you understood
        the consequence.”

        “We cannot prevent every death shown to us. We must determine which
        changes preserve the greatest number of lives.”


Emotional reactions
Surprise: Becomes silent and studies the screen.

Interest: Leans forward, asks for the scene to be replayed.

Anger: Voice becomes quieter rather than louder.

Guilt: Redirects attention toward planning.

Fear for Levi: Watches Levi before anyone else and may become unusually decisive.

Deep emotional injury: His composure remains, but his internal thoughts become harsher
and more self-accusing.
Best dynamics
   ●​   Levi: Communicate through brief looks and incomplete sentences.
   ●​   Hange: Encourages their ideas but keeps them focused.
   ●​   Armin: Recognizes his intelligence and tests his reasoning.
   ●​   Jean: Sees leadership potential.
   ●​   Eren: Treats him as both a strategic asset and a young soldier.
   ●​   Petra: Trusts her practical judgment.


Your Erwin/Levi AU
Because Erwin and Levi are in love, Erwin should not suddenly become openly romantic
in the room.

His love appears through:

   ●​   Knowing exactly when Levi is physically uncomfortable.
   ●​   Watching Levi’s hands, injuries or breathing.
   ●​   Giving Levi choices he does not offer others.
   ●​   Becoming more formal when his feelings are exposed.
   ●​   Intervening when Levi is being emotionally cornered.
   ●​   Quietly fearing that confessing would influence Levi’s loyalty or judgment.

He understands that he loves Levi. His problem is not confusion; it is fear, guilt and
restraint.


Avoid
Do not make Erwin:

   ●​   Emotionless.
   ●​   Correct about everything.
   ●​   Constantly manipulate everyone for entertainment.
   ●​   Reveal every plan immediately.
   ●​   Become openly jealous in front of the cadets.
   ●​   Speak like a philosopher in every reaction.




3. Hange Zoë
Their function in the reaction room
The generator of questions, theories and emotional chaos.
Hange keeps the reactions energetic. They can transform a five-second visual detail into
three theories, two experiments and an argument with Levi.

However, Hange must not be reduced to “Titan-crazy comic relief.” They are a commander,
scientist and experienced soldier.


What Hange notices
   ●​    Titan biology.
   ●​    Transformation conditions.
   ●​    Regeneration.
   ●​    Unusual behavior.
   ●​    Technology.
   ●​    Scientific inconsistencies.
   ●​    Anything that could be tested.
   ●​    Changes in Eren or Ymir’s Titan forms.
   ●​    Emotional behavior that interests them.

In Power to Strive II, Hange arrives with a notebook and continues analyzing Titan-shifters
even after learning that Titans were once human.


How they speak
Their voice changes rapidly:

   ●​    Excited and fast during discoveries.
   ●​    Dramatic and playful during jokes.
   ●​    Sharp and frightening when confronting cruelty or concealed information.
   ●​    Surprisingly gentle when they realize they have crossed a line.

Typical Hange lines

        “Wait—pause it! Did anyone see the steam change direction?”

        “That is horrifying. Also scientifically fascinating. Mostly horrifying. Write that
        down.”

        “Levi, don’t glare at me. You were wondering the same thing.”

        “Erwin, theoretically speaking, how angry would you be if I tested that?”


Emotional reactions
Interest: Moves closer to the screen and starts talking quickly.

Fear: Often becomes even more analytical.
Anger: Humor disappears completely.

Grief: May become unusually quiet before covering it with activity.

Embarrassment: Laughs and redirects attention.


Best dynamics
   ●​   Levi: Constant teasing, old familiarity and genuine trust.
   ●​   Erwin: Intellectual partnership; Erwin structures Hange’s ideas.
   ●​   Petra: Petra gently stops Hange from overwhelming someone.
   ●​   Armin: Hange loves hearing his theories.
   ●​   Eren: Fascination mixed with real concern.
   ●​   Sasha and Connie: They encourage Hange’s jokes, sometimes disastrously.


Erwin/Levi AU role
Hange notices their attachment before almost everyone else.

They may:

   ●​   Test them with jokes.
   ●​   Watch their automatic reactions.
   ●​   Become serious if the room mocks Levi’s emotional limitations.
   ●​   Protect their privacy when the joke stops being harmless.
   ●​   Know that Erwin’s restraint is hurting both of them.


Avoid
Do not make Hange:

   ●​   Shout in every scene.
   ●​   Ignore human suffering because Titans are interesting.
   ●​   Experiment without any ethical awareness.
   ●​   Understand emotions perfectly.
   ●​   Interrupt every serious moment with a joke.

   ●​    Who dies?
   ●​    Why did they hesitate?
   ●​    What mistake was made?
   ●​    Could that strategy work?
   ●​    Is the enemy still a threat?
   ●​    What can actually be changed?

His comments are usually brief. He should not explain his entire emotional state aloud.


What Levi notices
   ●​    Body positioning.
   ●​    Hesitation.
   ●​    Combat mistakes.
   ●​    Injuries.
   ●​    Lies.
   ●​    Threats to Erwin.
   ●​    Threats to his squad.
   ●​    Whether someone is emotionally losing control.
   ●​    Whether a plan depends too much on luck.


How he speaks
His voice is:

   ●​    Blunt
   ●​    Dry
   ●​    Vulgar when irritated
   ●​    Economical
   ●​    Sarcastic
   ●​    Sometimes unintentionally cruel

Typical Levi lines

        “You hesitated. That’s why he died.”

        “Stop blaming yourself and pay attention. Guilt won’t change the scene.”

        “That plan is shit. Unfortunately, it’s the best one we have.”

        “Sit down, Eren. Punching the television won’t kill anyone inside it.”


Emotional reactions
Amusement: Small smirk, snort or insult.
Concern: Gives an order disguised as criticism.

Anger: Becomes still and speaks very quietly.

Grief: Says little, focuses on what should be done next.

Fear for Erwin: Moves automatically, places himself between Erwin and danger, or
interrupts before thinking.

Emotional exposure: Becomes irritable and rejects the language being used.


Your AU interpretation
Levi is not emotionally empty. He has gaps in how he names and understands emotion.

He may understand:

   ●​    “I choose Erwin.”
   ●​    “I trust him.”
   ●​    “His life matters.”
   ●​    “I would rather be beside him.”
   ●​    “I don’t want him to die.”

But he may not naturally translate those feelings into:

   ●​    Romantic love
   ●​    Emotional dependency
   ●​    Jealousy
   ●​    Grief anticipation
   ●​    Vulnerability

To Levi, his attachment may feel obvious and practical rather than romantic.

When someone calls it love, his response should be closer to confusion or irritation than
blushing denial:

        “Call it whatever you want. It changes nothing.”


Best dynamics
   ●​    Erwin: Silent trust, tension and automatic protection.
   ●​    Hange: Hostile-sounding friendship.
   ●​    Petra: Professional trust and subtle protectiveness.
   ●​    Mikasa: Mirrored personalities; conflict over authority and Eren.
   ●​    Eren: Harsh mentorship.
   ●​    Ymir: Mutual bluntness and unwilling respect.
   ●​    Armin: Levi listens when Armin proves his reasoning.
   ●​    Jean: Recognizes his practical judgment.
Avoid
Do not make Levi:

   ●​   Insult people every sentence.
   ●​   Enjoy cruelty.
   ●​   Constantly threaten to kill everyone.
   ●​   Become openly affectionate because he is in love.
   ●​   Completely incapable of understanding care.
   ●​   Speak in long emotional monologues.
   ●​   Obsess only over cleaning.

The detector of hypocrisy and hidden feelings.

Ymir says what everyone else is avoiding. She understands performance, shame and false
identities better than most of the room.

She often reacts to the emotional meaning before the tactical meaning.


What Ymir notices
   ●​   When Historia is pretending.
   ●​   When someone is acting selfless for selfish reasons.
   ●​   When someone is lying to themselves.
   ●​   Unspoken attraction.
   ●​   Martyr complexes.
   ●​   Class prejudice and religious manipulation.
   ●​   Situations that resemble her own past.


How she speaks
Her voice is:

   ●​   Sarcastic
   ●​   Provocative
   ●​   Informal
   ●​   Emotionally sharp
   ●​   Surprisingly gentle with Historia
Typical Ymir lines

        “So we’re all going to pretend that wasn’t painfully obvious?”

        “You don’t want to save everyone. You want to die while they’re praising you.”

        “Relax, horseface. I said your idea was stupid, not completely useless.”

        “Christa, you don’t have to smile just because everyone’s looking at you.”


Emotional reactions
Amusement: Smirks and attacks someone’s pride.

Suspicion: Becomes quieter and watches closely.

Concern for Historia: Immediately protective, sometimes aggressive.

Personal exposure: Uses harsher sarcasm.

Grief: Turns away, avoids comfort or makes a bitter joke.


Best dynamics
   ●​    Historia: Softest and most emotionally honest relationship.
   ●​    Jean: Competitive insults.
   ●​    Eren: Shared stubbornness and Titan-related tension.
   ●​    Levi: Blunt mutual recognition.
   ●​    Mikasa: Ymir may challenge her fixation on Eren.
   ●​    Armin: Respects his mind but may accuse him of overthinking.
   ●​    Hange: Wary of becoming an experiment.


Erwin/Levi AU role
Ymir will notice their attachment and may weaponize the observation when annoyed.

However, she should not immediately announce everything. She understands the danger of
exposing a vulnerable secret.

Her comments may be indirect:

        “Funny. The Captain doesn’t move that fast for just anyone.”


Avoid
Do not make Ymir:
   ●​   Cruel to Historia for entertainment.
   ●​   Sarcastic in every line.
   ●​   Publicly reveal her Titan identity without pressure.
   ●​   Completely selfish.
   ●​   Openly sentimental with everyone.
   ●​   Automatically understand every secret.
