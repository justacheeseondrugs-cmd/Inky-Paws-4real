# Continuity notes

## Established invariants

1. Coriolanus Snow and Edmund Voss are one man. Voss is an acquired public identity, not another personality. [Snow VI]
2. Use young Tom Blyth Snow. Love and tenderness are real; so are possessiveness, entitlement and harm. Do not flatten him into either an innocent romantic hero or a caricature. [II]
3. The Underground relationship was genuinely mutual and happy for a meaningful period. Snow was more openly affectionate; Levi genuinely reciprocated. Do not retroactively make every past gesture a scheme. [III]
4. The breakup is about incompatible moral choices affecting others, not poverty, Levi's naivety or fear Snow would kill him. Its specific precipitating event remains open. [IV]
5. Levi could have killed Snow, spared him, provided a way aboveground and told him never to return. Levi meant a permanent goodbye. [IV.4]
6. Keep three peaceful village years followed by five years of deliberate reinvention. Snow is capable of a life that is not constant obsession. [V]
7. At Eren's trial both recognize each other immediately. Snow's public presence has a legitimate political rationale; details still need approval. [VII]
8. Erwin and Levi are NOT already a couple. Their growing closeness does not grant Snow a claim over Levi. [VII.4; XII.21–24]
9. Snow returns expecting renewed love, not revenge. Levi has moved forward and owes no renewed relationship. Past intimacy is not present consent. [I; VIII; X]
10. Avoid unlimited political authority, generic villain speeches or an opening backstory dump. [VI; IX; XI–XII]
