# Story bible

Source of AU-specific facts: [Snow AU original](archives/originals/1-Snow-AU-.pdf), titled **MASTER CHARACTER AND RELATIONSHIP DOSSIER — CORIOLANUS SNOW / EDMUND VOSS**. Section references below refer to that dossier. No finalized Snow chapter text was supplied.

## Premise and setting — ESTABLISHED

Coriolanus Snow, based on young Snow as portrayed by Tom Blyth in *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes*, exists within the world of *Attack on Titan*. Write neither elderly President Snow nor a generic omnipotent villain. How he came to exist in this setting is **UNRESOLVED**; the dossier does not establish a transport mechanism or import Panem's institutions. [I–II, pp. 1–4]

Snow and Levi were genuine lovers and lived together in the Underground. Their life included safety, mutual competence, affection and ordinary domestic happiness. Snow was the more openly affectionate partner; Levi welcomed and reciprocated his touch. Their relationship was not manipulation from the outset. [III, pp. 4–8]

Snow's growing ruthlessness and willingness to sacrifice others made their values incompatible. Levi did not fear Snow would kill him. In their final confrontation Levi could have killed him, but could not bring himself to do it. Levi provided a way aboveground and explicitly told Snow never to return. Levi meant a permanent breakup; Snow failed to accept that permanence. [IV, pp. 8–10]

The present reunion occurs eight years after the separation. Snow spent the first three years living peacefully in a village, then learned Levi was alive and known as Captain Levi, Humanity's Strongest Soldier. He spent the following five years building influence and a respectable identity, **Edmund Voss**. Voss is the same person, not another personality. [I; V–VI, pp. 1–2, 11–15]

Voss is a political adviser and representative connected to the Crown's administrative circles. His influence comes from competence, reputation and connections, not unlimited command over the Crown or military. His exact office and responsibilities require approval. [VI, pp. 13–15; XIII]

He and Levi reunite at Eren Yeager's military trial, which Snow attends for legitimate political reasons. Both recognize each other immediately. Snow is genuinely delighted; Levi's recognition is subtle but certain. Erwin and Hange can notice his reaction without knowing its cause. [VII, pp. 15–18]

Erwin and Levi **are not a couple**. They have considerable trust and growing emotional closeness, with no established romantic relationship. Snow can admire and respect Erwin before interpreting him as a rival. [VII.4; XII.21–23]

## Emotional and narrative rules — ESTABLISHED

The dossier's central statement is preserved verbatim:

> Snow believes that he and Levi have a future.
>
> Levi believes that they had a past.

[X, pp. 22–23]

Snow returns because he believes he is coming home, not to exact revenge. His love, warmth, possessiveness and capacity for harm coexist. Greater money and status do not demonstrate that he understands or has repaired his moral failures. Levi is not waiting for him and owes neither forgiveness nor renewed intimacy. Old consent does not confer present permission. [I–II; VIII; X]

The watching-room story begins **immediately before Eren's trial**. Viewers learn through footage, selective revelations by Candy and their own observations. Private thoughts and memories may be revealed selectively; no single opening exposition dump should explain everything. [VII.1; XI, pp. 23–24]

The general character bible's starting point, approximately two days before the 57th Expedition, conflicts with this AU-specific starting point and is superseded for Snow. Preserve each character's actual personal knowledge; never treat dossier text, reference fiction or another character's memories as shared room knowledge.
