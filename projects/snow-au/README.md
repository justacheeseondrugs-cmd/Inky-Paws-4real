## Status labels

- **ESTABLISHED**: explicitly stated in the supplied authoritative dossier or current author instructions. A dossier fact is not necessarily already narrated or known by the room.
- **PROPOSED**: an unapproved option, including suggestions made inside a source document.
- **UNRESOLVED**: missing information or a decision awaiting author approval.
- **DISCARDED**: explicitly rejected. Do not infer this status from age, filename or difference alone.

The source PDFs stay byte-identical under `archives/originals/`. Text under `archives/extracted/` is a working extraction, not a substitute for original formatting. Summaries cite Snow dossier section numbers and PDF page numbers. No ZIP or other compressed container was supplied; the word “archives” here includes the three PDFs.

## Important limits

**UNRESOLVED:** Snow AU Chapter One and any later approved drafts were not supplied. No existing approved chapter was rewritten, but preservation of an unavailable Chapter One cannot be verified. The first chapter of *Power to Strive* belongs to another author's reference work.

This folder is independent of `/workspace/inky-paws`. The app's Original, Kael, Violet, A Day Off and Your Name material is not imported as Snow canon. The public destination is `justacheeseondrugs-cmd/Inky-Paws-4real`, branch `main`, under `projects/snow-au/`. Publication adds reference files only; it does not install them into browser storage or change the app. The GitHub commit records the version history.
